## 1. Research purpose and claim boundary

The study replaces the unrun five-class, 1,500-sample candidate formal stage
with a larger closed-world experiment. It measures the correctness, cost, and
website-traffic-classification resistance of all seven implemented QCSD
research-defence modes over 100 prepared public-page classes. The eight formal
modes are an
undefended baseline, FRONT, Tamaraw, Traffic Morphing, WTF-PAD,
Walkie-Talkie, BuFLO, and canonical CTSP CS-BuFLO.

This is a client-only QUIC study using ordinary HTTP/3 servers. It is not a
bilateral reproduction of the TCP/Tor protocols in the original studies. Its
permitted candidate description remains **“validated client-only QCSD
adaptation”**, and only after a final attestation verifies. Until then, the
authoritative wording is **“five validated defences plus two candidates / nine
selectable modes”**. `undefended` and `static` are baseline/control modes;
they are not research defences.

The endpoint uses one frozen set of 100 classes for every condition: each
class receives the same 20 visits under each of the eight formal modes,
yielding 100 × 20 × 8 = 16,000 authoritative samples. Before that collection,
every selected class must pass one first-launch execution under every one of
the nine selectable modes, including `static`, yielding the separate 900-cell
compatibility certification.

The class scale is methodologically aligned with prior website-fingerprinting
work without claiming dataset equivalence. QCSD evaluated 100 monitored
pages, BuFLO used privacy sets up to 128 pages, and CS-BuFLO's principal
experiment used 200 sites with 20 visits. Those studies differ in transport,
endpoint cooperation, page population, observation layer, and classifier
protocol, so their numeric results are contextual rather than QCSD acceptance
ranges: [QCSD](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/sec22-smith.pdf),
[BuFLO](https://rist.tech.cornell.edu/papers/trafanal.pdf), and
[CS-BuFLO](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1401.6022).

Two broader methodology results shape the class contract. Panchenko et al.
distinguish individual **webpages** from whole **websites** and show why small
homepage-only universes do not establish Internet-scale behaviour; this study
therefore claims 100 exact prepared page classes, samples across five rank
strata, and does not generalise a class to an entire site. Yan and Kaur show
that informative traffic features change with the observer's available header
view; this study consequently preregisters one narrow observer frame and
forbids class selection based on separability instead of searching for a
favourable feature set: [Website Fingerprinting at Internet Scale](https://www.ndss-symposium.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/website-fingerprinting-internet-scale.pdf)
and [Feature Selection for Website Fingerprinting](https://petsymposium.org/popets/2018/popets-2018-0039.pdf).

### 2.2 Exact page admission

One class is one exact prepared public page associated with one unique frozen
Tranco candidate domain, not every possible state of a live website. That
candidate domain is itself the navigation boundary: the exact host and its
subdomains are in bounds; a parent, sibling, or otherwise related domain is
not. The protocol does not use a Public Suffix List to widen or reinterpret
that frozen boundary. This restriction identifies the primary page and its
redirects; it does **not** impose a same-origin restriction on subresources.
The discovery pass iteratively admits observed public HTTPS GET origins, up to
32 approved origins and 512 audited origins over eight convergence passes,
then preparation requires rendered coverage from every approved origin.
Observed non-GET and non-HTTPS requests remain audited exclusions outside the
replay construct; their presence alone does not reject the class or permit an
otherwise eligible resource to be omitted. Document-navigation origins are
seeded per page, so an origin observed while checking one optional page cannot
silently constrain another page's graph. Single-origin and naturally
multi-origin pages are both eligible. Origin count is neither a quota nor a
selection input, and no acquisition or selection stage may intentionally omit
a multi-origin resource or class. The realised final-cohort origin-count
distribution must be reported. The formal handoff's verifier-bound
`dataset.json` records the per-class origin count, the origin-count histogram,
and the single-/multi-origin totals under `resource_origin_profile`; it imposes
no artificial minimum number of multi-origin classes. A realised cohort that
contains no multi-origin classes is acceptable only if the precommitted
ordering and technical gates produce that result naturally; origin count may
never be used to omit a candidate. Conversely, an accepted multi-origin
class retains its complete converged approved-origin and public-HTTPS-GET
resource graph. The 32-approved-origin, 512-audited-origin, and eight-pass
limits are fail-closed admission bounds, not truncation settings: exceeding
any limit or failing to converge rejects the prospective class, and no
accepted workload silently drops a discovered eligible public HTTPS GET
subresource. The fresh browser discovery performed during final preparation
must reproduce the converged origin set: a newly observed unapproved HTTPS GET is a recoverable
probe failure that forces reconvergence on the next attempt, and persisted
complete-coverage evidence independently rejects any `origin not approved`
exclusion.

depends on a service worker.

The page must:

- be a public HTTPS GET with no authentication, account state, CAPTCHA,
  service-worker dependency, or cross-domain redirect alias;
- have an explicit, frozen set of approved HTTPS origins and a reproducible
  dependency graph;
- complete through Neqo/HTTP/3 with exact response status, byte count, body
  SHA-256, final URL, and prepared-workload SHA-256;
- remain within one MiB per resource, the 1,200-byte UDP-payload ceiling, and
  the existing client/capture limits; and
- produce fitting-eligible natural traffic in both directions.

Origin status and count never affect eligibility ranking, stratum quota, or
cohort membership. Later exact endpoint-origin-set overlap is only a scheduling
input that reorders already-selected workload/visit groups for cooldown
separation; it is not a hidden multi-origin selection criterion.

Complete-graph preservation is rechecked after acquisition rather than merely
assumed from the class label. For every accepted fitting,
pilot-compatibility, certification, canary, and formal sample, the verifier
rederives the runtime manifest from the admitted prepared manifest and requires
the run receipt to contain the exact complete response set and canonical
endpoint-origin set. It also reopens the campaign, application/runtime hashes,
qualified chaff, mode-appropriate parameter/provenance inputs, seed, request
policy, and launch limits. This single contract covers all nine selectable
modes. Defence-specific application-resource filtering and projection of a
multi-origin workload to one origin are forbidden.

The canonical homepage is considered first. If it is unsuitable, at most four
query-free HTML links within the exact candidate-domain boundary, discovered
from the homepage, may be tested in deterministic hash order. Authentication,
account, administration, cart, checkout, payment, search, and session paths
are rejected by a fixed token policy. Every examined page and machine-readable
rejection reason remains in the attrition ledger; there is no concealed manual
shortlist.

### 2.3 Longitudinal stability gate

Every prospective page must pass three observations initiated from one baseline.
The registered window applies to the durably checkpointed network-observation
start, while `probe_completed_at` separately records completion. A retry may
start only inside the same window and receives its actual retry timestamp; it
cannot borrow the interrupted attempt's timestamp. Every retry has a unique,
durably checkpointed attempt ID and create-only prepared manifest; an orphan
written by an interrupted attempt can never be reused or relabelled as a later
retry. This start-window definition is necessary because complete discovery
plus three-run Neqo preparation is a bounded multi-step observation rather
than an instantaneous sample.

| Probe | Admissible elapsed-time window |
|---|---:|
| `t+30s` | 25–35 seconds |
| `t+24h` | 23 h 45 min–24 h 15 min |
| `t+72h` | 71 h 45 min–72 h 15 min |

All three observations must agree on final URL, status, content type, body
length, body SHA-256, and the semantic prepared-replay identity recorded as
`resource_graph_sha256`. That identity hashes the preparation source/final URL,
approved origins, complete expected-response identities, and the full Neqo
runtime manifest. It deliberately excludes per-run packet-qualification and
provenance fields.

Each observation's raw `prepared_workload_sha256` is still recorded, but raw
manifest hashes are not compared longitudinally because those manifests carry
per-run evidence. Once all three semantic identities pass, the raw manifest
captured by the first `t+30s` observation is the one immutably admitted as the
class workload; neither the `t+24h` nor `t+72h` manifest may replace it. The
long windows tolerate runner scheduling and host suspension without permitting
an arbitrary later observation to be relabelled. All stability and terminal
evidence is create-only.

### 2.4 Pilot, final cohort, and reserves

The first 24 technically eligible, hash-ordered candidates in each stratum
form the 120-class pilot. The final cohort contains exactly 20 per stratum and
the remaining four per stratum form 20 ordered reserves.

Final selection may use only:

1. preregistered boolean technical eligibility; and
2. an optional graph indicating whether two classes can form a feasible
   Walkie-Talkie pair.

For v1, that optional graph is deliberately sparse. Its only edges are the 60
one-to-one WT6 runtime profiles present in the deeply verified, finalised pilot
bundle, and an edge is admitted only when frozen prefix and sidecar evidence
proves sufficient capacity at both endpoints. The other 7,080 unordered pilot
pairs are treated as unqualified, not inferred feasible from endpoint-only
compatibility. Final selection must find 50 of those qualified edges while
retaining exactly 20 classes per rank stratum; if it cannot, v1 stops before a
final cohort is frozen.

Classifier accuracy, trace separability, bandwidth overhead, latency, and
privacy outcomes are prohibited selection inputs. If the feasible-pair graph
is supplied, the final 100 must have a deterministic perfect matching. The 20
reserves are a frozen attrition/planning inventory, not an automatic
substitution queue, and no predecessor receipt is mutated in place.

A create-only successor decision can be reconstructed only from a sealed,
incomplete, exact 900-cell first-launch certification. The sole authorising
failure is an `undefended` `fidelity`/`StrictPreparedResponseIdentityFailure`
whose details name that same cell's `workload_id`. The identical typed,
same-workload failure under a defended mode is admissible only as
non-authorising corroboration for a class already carrying that undefended
proof. It cannot add a replacement class. A `StrictDefenseFidelityFailure`, a
`StrictClientDefenseExecutionFailure`, a defended-only or unrelated prepared-
identity failure, every other correctness failure, and any capture,
interruption, infrastructure, or unclassified failure block the whole
decision.

A defence or QCSD fidelity failure at regression, controlled qualification,
fitting, qualification, compatibility, certification, canary, or formal time
is therefore a release-blocking implementation defect to retain, diagnose, and
repair on the client. It never authorises dropping or relabelling
the class, activating a reserve, removing an origin or resource, weakening an
acceptance or fidelity threshold, or selecting/fitting around the failure.
Ordinary HTTP/3 servers remain unchanged: a repair may alter the client-side
defence, controller, transport integration, runner, or Lab validation path,
but it may not introduce a symmetric or server-side defence deployment. If no
client-only correction can satisfy the unchanged contract, the stage remains
blocked and the limitation is reported.

Any client-side code correction starts a new create-only immutable cohort with
a new source/image binding. The build, reference, regression, code, and
controlled foundation gates are rerun, as are all downstream fitting,
qualification, compatibility, certification, canary, and formal pairings
invalidated by the changed source. No result bound to the predecessor source
may be spliced into the replacement pairing. The sole class-replacement path
remains the producer-recomputed, same-class `undefended`
`StrictPreparedResponseIdentityFailure` described above.

Successors are generational. Every generation retains the original frozen
120-class pilot and qualified one-to-one 60-edge graph as selection authority;
binds the exact immediate predecessor cohort, assembly, final selection,
foundation, source, build, and restart lineage; permanently excludes the
cumulative union of all proven failed classes; maximises retention from the
immediate predecessor; and receives a distinct hash-derived study and launch
namespace. Each generation must acquire fresh 2,000-sample authoritative
fitting, 600-execution final qualification, and 900-cell first-launch
certification evidence. Predecessor outputs cannot be reused. Quota or reserve
exhaustion fails closed for a new acquisition cohort, and replacement is
forbidden after authorised formal capture starts.

Successor selection does not alter page admission. An activated reserve keeps
the exact admitted prepared workload and its complete approved-origin and
resource graph from the original acquisition-assembly lineage. Single-origin
and naturally multi-origin classes remain eligible, origin count remains
neither a replacement input nor a quota, and projecting the graph to fewer
origins or resources is forbidden. Before an incomplete certification can
authorise a successor decision, every accepted cell under every mode must also
pass the complete run-graph check; BuFLO and CS-BuFLO then receive their
additional current-schema chronology validation. Failed prepared-identity
cells remain subject to exact producer recomputation from their frozen
workload and retained first attempt.

### 2.5 Mandatory selection and assembly receipts

Class admission produces two co-required, create-only receipts:

1. The **selection receipt** records the exact ordered candidate, pilot, final,
   and reserve inventories, boolean eligibility, optional feasible-pair graph,
   and selected perfect matching.
2. The **cohort assembly receipt** has receipt type
   `qcsd-class-study-cohort-assembly`. It binds the selection receipt to the
   candidate catalogue and complete per-candidate admission ledger. For every
   admitted class it records the selected page, stability-receipt hash, and
   the path and exact SHA-256 of the raw `t+30s` prepared workload admitted
   after three-window semantic stability succeeds.

The selection receipt alone is insufficient to run a campaign: it identifies
which classes were chosen but does not prove which prepared bytes implement
those identities. Every campaign YAML therefore carries both
`class_study_cohort` and `class_study_cohort_assembly`. Result freezing copies
them as `inputs/class-study-cohort.json` and
`inputs/class-study-cohort-assembly.json`. Fitting, result verification,
handoff, and evaluation independently require both hashes and reject a
prepared workload whose bytes differ from the assembly receipt.

The later post-compatibility final-selection input is a separate, create-only
schema-2 receipt. It binds the exact qualification authority used to prepare
and collect the pilot evidence, and its publisher independently recomputes the
selection from the exact pilot fitting result and compatibility result before
publication. The earlier schema-1 shape was pre-publication and
non-evidentiary; current validators reject it explicitly rather than accepting
it as a legacy evidence format.

lookup_result_root() {
  # Exit 0 and print the sole canonical checkpoint root when present; exit 3
  # when no experiment has been created; use every other status for corruption.
  python3 - "$RESULTS_ROOT" "$1" "$2" <<'PY'
import json
import os
from pathlib import Path
import sys

results = Path(sys.argv[1])
campaign_name = sys.argv[2]
expected_role = sys.argv[3]
if Path(campaign_name).name != campaign_name or not campaign_name.startswith(
    "classifier-multiorigin100-v1-"
):
    raise SystemExit("result lookup received a non-canonical campaign name")
if results.is_symlink() or not results.is_dir():
    raise SystemExit(f"canonical results root is unsafe or absent: {results}")
parent = results / campaign_name
if not parent.exists():
    raise SystemExit(3)
if parent.is_symlink() or not parent.is_dir():
    raise SystemExit(f"campaign result parent is unsafe: {parent}")

experiments = []
for child in sorted(parent.iterdir(), key=lambda path: path.name):
    experiment = child / "experiment.json"
    if experiment.is_symlink():
        raise SystemExit(f"experiment checkpoint is a symlink: {experiment}")
    if experiment.exists():
        if child.is_symlink() or not child.is_dir() or not experiment.is_file():
            raise SystemExit(f"experiment checkpoint root is unsafe: {child}")
        experiments.append(experiment)
if not experiments:
    # A launch claim may have materialised only reproducible inputs before the
    # durable experiment write. The fresh-capture path validates and recovers
    # that exact case; this lookup must not invent a resume root for it.
    raise SystemExit(3)
if len(experiments) != 1:
    raise SystemExit(
        f"expected exactly one experiment below {parent}, found {len(experiments)}"
    )

experiment_path = experiments[0]
try:
    value = json.loads(experiment_path.read_text(encoding="utf-8"))
except (OSError, UnicodeError, json.JSONDecodeError) as error:
    raise SystemExit(f"cannot read experiment checkpoint {experiment_path}: {error}")
configuration = value.get("configuration") if isinstance(value, dict) else None
root = experiment_path.parent
if (
    not isinstance(configuration, dict)
    or value.get("name") != campaign_name
    or value.get("run_id") != root.name
    or value.get("status") not in {"running", "complete", "incomplete"}
    or configuration.get("class_study_id") != "classifier-multiorigin100-v1"
    or configuration.get("evidence_role") != expected_role
):
    raise SystemExit(f"experiment identity does not match {campaign_name}: {experiment_path}")
canonical_parent = (results.resolve() / campaign_name)
if root.resolve() != canonical_parent / root.name:
    raise SystemExit(f"experiment result path is not canonical: {root}")
print(os.fspath(root))
PY
}

optional_result_root() {
  local campaign_name="$1" expected_role="$2" destination_name="$3"
  local discovered="" status
  if discovered="$(lookup_result_root "$campaign_name" "$expected_role")"; then
    printf -v "$destination_name" '%s' "$discovered"
    return 0
  else
    status=$?
  fi
  if (( status == 3 )); then
    printf -v "$destination_name" '%s' ""
    return 0
  fi
  return "$status"
}

rehydrate_capture_state() {
  local block_number block canary_name formal_name canary_result formal_result
  local previous_formal="" gap=0

  optional_result_root "$PILOT_FITTING_NAME" pilot-fitting PILOT_FITTING_RESULT
  optional_result_root \
    "$PILOT_COMPATIBILITY_NAME" pilot-compatibility PILOT_COMPATIBILITY_RESULT
  optional_result_root \
    "$AUTHORITATIVE_FITTING_NAME" authoritative-fitting AUTHORITATIVE_FITTING_RESULT
  optional_result_root "$CERTIFICATION_NAME" certification CERTIFICATION_RESULT

  [[ -z "$PILOT_COMPATIBILITY_RESULT" || -n "$PILOT_FITTING_RESULT" ]] || {
    echo "pilot compatibility exists without the pilot fitting checkpoint" >&2
    return 1
  }
  [[ -z "$AUTHORITATIVE_FITTING_RESULT" || -n "$PILOT_COMPATIBILITY_RESULT" ]] || {
    echo "authoritative fitting exists without pilot compatibility" >&2
    return 1
  }
  [[ -z "$CERTIFICATION_RESULT" || -n "$AUTHORITATIVE_FITTING_RESULT" ]] || {
    echo "certification exists without authoritative fitting" >&2
    return 1
  }

  REHYDRATED_CANARY_RESULTS=()
  REHYDRATED_FORMAL_RESULTS=()
  CANARY_RESULT_ARGS=()
  FORMAL_RESULT_ARGS=()
  REHYDRATED_FORMAL_PREREQUISITE_ARGS=()
  if [[ -n "$CERTIFICATION_RESULT" ]]; then
    REHYDRATED_FORMAL_PREREQUISITE_ARGS+=(--result "$CERTIFICATION_RESULT")
  fi
  previous_formal="$CERTIFICATION_RESULT"
  for block_number in {1..10}; do
    printf -v block '%02d' "$block_number"
    canary_name="classifier-multiorigin100-v1-canary-${block}-1200"
    formal_name="classifier-multiorigin100-v1-formal-${block}-1200"
    optional_result_root "$canary_name" canary canary_result
    optional_result_root "$formal_name" formal formal_result
    if (( gap )) && [[ -n "$canary_result" || -n "$formal_result" ]]; then
      echo "capture chain has a result after the first absent canary/formal pair" >&2
      return 1
    fi
    [[ -z "$canary_result" || -n "$previous_formal" ]] || {
      echo "canary block $block exists without its ordered predecessor" >&2
      return 1
    }
    [[ -z "$formal_result" || -n "$canary_result" ]] || {
      echo "formal block $block exists without its same-block canary" >&2
      return 1
    }
    if [[ -n "$canary_result" ]]; then
      REHYDRATED_CANARY_RESULTS["$block"]="$canary_result"
      CANARY_RESULT_ARGS+=(--canary-result "$canary_result")
      REHYDRATED_FORMAL_PREREQUISITE_ARGS+=(--result "$canary_result")
    fi
    if [[ -n "$formal_result" ]]; then
      REHYDRATED_FORMAL_RESULTS["$block"]="$formal_result"
      FORMAL_RESULT_ARGS+=(--formal-result "$formal_result")
      REHYDRATED_FORMAL_PREREQUISITE_ARGS+=(--result "$formal_result")
      previous_formal="$formal_result"
    else
      gap=1
      previous_formal=""
    fi
  done
}

capture_or_resume() {
  local campaign_name="$1" expected_role="$2" campaign="$3"
  local existing="" status
  shift 3

  if existing="$(lookup_result_root "$campaign_name" "$expected_role")"; then
    printf 'resuming claimed %s result: %s\n' "$campaign_name" "$existing"
    ./qcsd-lab class-study resume \
      "$@" \
      --capture-result "$existing" \
      --foundation-attestation "$FOUNDATION" \
      --execute
  else
    status=$?
    (( status == 3 )) || return "$status"
    printf 'capturing previously unclaimed campaign: %s\n' "$campaign_name"
    ./qcsd-lab class-study capture \
      "$@" \
      --campaign "$campaign" \
      --results-root "$RESULTS_ROOT" \
      --foundation-attestation "$FOUNDATION" \
      --build-execution-receipt "$BUILD" \
      --execute
  fi

  if ! CAPTURE_RESULT="$(lookup_result_root "$campaign_name" "$expected_role")"; then
    echo "capture command returned without one canonical experiment root: $campaign_name" >&2
    return 1
  fi
}

Every command above is prerequisite ordered. `fit-numeric`, `prefix-specs`,
`qualify-prefix`, and fitting-directory `verify` consume the exact completed
fitting result through `--capture-result`; `finalize-fitting` binds that same
result through `--result`; and qualification is resumable only at its named
checkpoint and temporary sidecar root. If live qualification is
interrupted without a typed client/QCSD failure, rerun the identical
`qualify-prefix --qualify-all-pending` command. Its atomic publication creates
the canonical named set only after every workload passes.

Fresh `capture --execute` requires `--build-execution-receipt` and derives the
exact collection image from it. A capture that creates `experiment.json` but
does not complete must be resumed, never relaunched. `capture_or_resume` calls
`lookup_result_root` first: status 3 is the only state that selects fresh
`capture`; one canonical checkpoint selects `resume`; ambiguity or malformed
identity is terminal. Both paths receive the same admission, foundation, and
ordered `--result` prerequisites. Resume deliberately does not take a new build
receipt: it derives the collection image from the frozen
`inputs/study-environment.json`. Capture freezes its campaign, workloads,
defence inputs, qualification evidence, cohort receipts, and authorities under
the claimed result's `inputs/` directory. Re-running the prologue is therefore
mandatory after opening a new shell; manually copying a previous shell's array
or choosing `capture` by eye is forbidden.

That frozen result directory is not, by itself, a portable evidence archive.
Its result-local foundation copy may be relocated only under the narrowly
validated path allowance, while the foundation's hash-bound build, gate, and
result paths remain canonical external prerequisites that must still exist for
deep verification. Copying a result tree without those transitive authorities
therefore fails closed. Portability is supplied only by the separately
verified, checksum-closed handoff, which copies the required bound authorities
and evidence while preserving their exact digests.

After certification returns complete, continue in the same rehydrated shell.
The readiness command deliberately receives both cohort admissions, every
foundation receipt/result family, the pilot numeric fit, both completed fitting
captures, the finalised authoritative bundle, the published final sidecars and
prefixes, and the exact 900-cell certification result.

## 6. Compatibility and bounded-attempt semantics

The 900-cell final certification is the decisive pre-formal guarantee. Every
final class must pass exactly once under each of the nine selectable modes on
the exact frozen image, parameters, workload, fitting bundle, qualification
set, selection receipt, assembly receipt, and acceptance rules that formal
capture will use.

Each cell must prove:

- exact prepared status, redirect policy, body length, and body SHA-256;
- no lost, duplicated, reordered, or chaff-derived application bytes;
- valid workload-specific fitted parameter and chaff/prefix bindings;
- non-trivial, internally reconciled defence activity;
- no protocol error, deadlock, timeout leak, capture truncation, unexplained
  endpoint, oversized UDP payload, or timestamp mismatch; and
- exact schedule/event/packet and receive-credit terminal accounting.

BuFLO additionally permits no partial, suppressed, missed, late, mismatched,
catch-up, or unresolved event. CS-BuFLO permits partial/suppressed egress only
with matching congestion/pacing evidence and permits no actual missed or
unresolved event.

Failure of one of these defence-specific checks invokes the client-side repair
contract in Section 2.4; it does not turn the affected class into an attrition
candidate. An evidence-level violation is a `StrictDefenseFidelityFailure`; a
typed client runner/QCSD execution error is a
`StrictClientDefenseExecutionFailure`. Either is terminal: the exact failed
attempt and diagnostics remain sealed, the campaign stops before retrying or
launching another cell, and resume may only verify and preserve that incomplete
checkpoint. Capture restarts only under the appropriate newly gated immutable
cohort after a client-side repair. Durable verification rejects any later
attempt after either terminal type.

The implementation has two durable boundaries. First, before result
materialisation, it creates one global `qcsd-class-study-first-launch-claim`
for the canonical campaign role/block and cohort/assembly identity. That claim
fixes a single result root. A hard stop may recreate only recognised frozen
inputs at that same root while no `experiment.json` exists; it cannot open a
new launch namespace. The receipt's `first-launch` name refers to this single
campaign-result namespace; it does not mean that every canary or formal sample
is limited to one collector attempt.

Second, a physical sample launch begins when the runner marks the sample
`running`, increments its durable attempt count, and atomically checkpoints
`experiment.json` **before** calling the collector. A stop after that
checkpoint consumes the launch even if no later `attempt.json` was written.
Certification cells permit exactly one such launch. Canary and formal cells
permit at most three started attempts so a transient collector or network
failure cannot irrecoverably invalidate a 16,000-sample campaign. Every failed
attempt remains sealed; only a sample that passes the unchanged acceptance
rules can enter the handoff. There is no deletion, substitution, or post-hoc
relabelling. If `experiment.json` exists, execution must resume the globally
claimed result; the file is the sole sample-state and resume authority. A
failed certification cell invalidates v1. Only an undefended same-workload
`StrictPreparedResponseIdentityFailure` supplies successor authority; the same
typed, same-workload defended failure may corroborate that already-authorised
class, while every other failure blocks the decision. A canary or formal cell
that exhausts all three attempts blocks its campaign, and no replacement is
permitted after formal capture starts. Later public-page drift likewise fails
closed rather than refreshing the prepared identity or activating a reserve
in place.

The bounded retry allowance applies only to operational capture, transient
network, and other explicitly retryable failures. It never masks a defence or
QCSD defect. Every prerequisite-ordered class-study capture and resume must run
through the `class-study` coordinator, which verifies the prerequisite ledger
and grants a process-local capability bound to the exact campaign, cohort,
assembly, and successor identity. Generic `run` or `resume` cannot launch these
roles directly.

The resulting guarantee is deliberately bounded:

> Every selected class and selectable mode was independently demonstrated
> compatible with the exact frozen client, image, workload, parameter, and
> qualification cohort at the first-launch certification checkpoint that must
> precede formal capture.

It is not a guarantee that an uncontrolled public server will never change.

## 7. Formal acquisition and temporal evaluation

Each of ten chronological blocks contains 1,600 samples: 100 classes, two
visits per class, and eight modes. The complete formal corpus therefore has
16,000 samples and 2,000 samples per mode. Mode order uses a deterministic
cyclic Latin square. An origin-aware scheduling window interleaves unrelated
classes while preserving the mandatory 30-second cooldown for every approved
origin. Exact endpoint-origin-set overlap only reorders already-selected
workload/visit groups; it cannot alter eligibility, ranking, stratum quota, or
cohort membership.

Before every canary or formal launch or resume, current source deep-verifies the
exact sealed, closed-checksum 900-cell certification and derives its bytes per
sample and observed seconds per sample from that evidence alone. It projects
the remaining canary-plus-formal inventory after completed and eligible in-
progress samples, and requires free space equal to at least three times the
projected remaining evidence. Its conservative time estimate is the greater of
1.5 times the certification-rate projection and the registered
120-second-timeout + 30-second-origin-cooldown + one-second-settle allowance per
remaining sample. There is no class-study rehearsal stage and no rehearsal
result can satisfy this preflight. This campaign-scale reserve is independent
of the fixed 64 GiB backing-volume floor for a fresh evidentiary build; neither
gate substitutes for the other.

Before each block, all 100 classes must pass that block's strictly preceding
bounded-attempt undefended canary. “Preceding” is the executable ordering
contract; no unregistered maximum elapsed interval is claimed. A failed canary
prevents the block from starting. `experiment.json`
