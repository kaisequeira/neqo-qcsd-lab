## Actual startup and unchanged active cells

Fixed BuFLO continues to emit shaped outgoing opportunities from tick zero:
1,200 byte cells every 20 ms with the unchanged 5 ms outgoing deadline. Incoming
opportunities begin at the first 20 ms grid point strictly after the controller
observes both an actual qualified chaff request's terminal ACK and at least
1,200 bytes of exact eligible receive capacity. Active incoming opportunities
then retain complete 1,200 byte cells every 20 ms and the v8 10 ms incoming
release window. The other four modes gain no BuFLO marker or startup rule.

The native run must contain the exact seven-field source-bound V2 marker and a
separate actual `buflo_summary.incoming_startup` receipt. Suppressed startup
opportunities have no incoming schedule rows and supply no receive credit or
realized bytes. Their count must equal the recorded first incoming target
divided by the period. This policy authorizes no preclock unshaped transfer,
invented ACK, fake capacity or promotion of a missed active cell.

The [capture validator](../src/qcsd_lab/capture_acceptance_policy.py) reopens
`events.csv` and `schedule.csv`. Each selected-stream ACK reduction event must
match the original ACK payload, production sequence and production time. Its
actual controller clock supplies the ACK time; a fitted packet clock does not.
The validator rederives contiguous coverage of the complete positive request
extent plus FIN, retains the first terminal coverage time, checks the unique
ready event against the complete startup receipt, and binds the request to an
eligible frozen auxiliary resource. Incoming targets must start at the recorded
arm time, outgoing targets at zero, and both actual target sets must retain
their complete active cadence through the same final target.

The [normal fidelity path](../src/qcsd_lab/fidelity.py) rederives these startup
facts before applying the count offset. It records the actual incoming count,
the reopened event hash, and both the historical 5 ms violation count and the
prospective 10 ms release measurements. Accepted samples independently reopen
the source binding during [deep verification](../src/qcsd_lab/verification.py).
Marker-only, unarmed, mismatched-source or incomplete ACK evidence fails.
