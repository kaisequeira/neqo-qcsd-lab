# Canary control facts in one action

The typed qualification control authority reuses its unchanged original
validator and complete raw dependency binding only within one owning public
action. The identity includes the exact reference and consumer role document;
declaration seeds, another consumer, another artifact and another action do not
share a semantic result. Canonical, image, policy and chronology checks remain
active. Historical qualification and delivery validators retain their bytes.

The schema3 canary bridge similarly reuses its first complete derivation and
authenticated transport roots. Canary dependency registration happens once per
exact reference/runtime pair. Registration completes before a binding can be
reused, and exceptions never mark a partial binding complete.

These are in-memory control facts, with no persistent receipt or measurement
equivalence. The owner must call `OperationFacts.check()` before effects,
after ownership-lock waits and before returning success. That fence reopens
all registered bytes, full permission modes and complete directory membership,
including originally absent optional trees. Public launch retains its actual
installed image check and fresh intent/effect boundaries.

The focused80-campaign HOST fixture runs the real renderer and typed reader
with explicitly synthetic original proof/runtime boundaries. Its counts show
bounded validator/dependency selection in that action; they do not establish
installed startup speed, capture success or scientific credit. Source-only
comparison preserves the original measurement/qualification/Native groups.
